# ClearVision Consulting
# Content Guide

Version 1.0

---

# Purpose

This guide defines how ClearVision Consulting communicates across all digital products.

The goal is to create a consistent, trustworthy and premium communication style.

Every page should educate before it sells.

Every sentence should increase trust.

---

# Content Philosophy

People do not visit our website to admire design.

They visit because they have a problem.

Content should help them understand

• their problem

• our solution

• why they can trust us

Design supports communication.

Content creates value.

---

# Writing Style

Write like an experienced consultant.

Not like a marketing agency.

Use

- clear language
- precise wording
- short sentences
- active voice
- educational explanations

Avoid

- hype
- exaggerated promises
- empty marketing phrases
- unnecessary technical jargon
- complicated wording

---

# Tone of Voice

Professional

Calm

Confident

Helpful

Respectful

Educational

Trustworthy

Never

Aggressive

Pushy

Arrogant

Overly emotional

---

# Headlines

Headlines should answer questions.

Good examples

Improve digital workflows.

Precision for every treatment plan.

Mentoring that supports your clinical decisions.

Digital expertise for modern dentistry.

Avoid

Welcome to...

We are...

Leading provider...

World class...

Revolutionary...

---

# Paragraphs

Keep paragraphs short.

Prefer

2–4 sentences.

Break complex ideas into smaller sections.

Avoid walls of text.

---

# Calls to Action

CTAs should invite.

Never pressure.

Preferred examples

Schedule a consultation

Let's discuss your workflow

Explore our services

Discover how we can support your practice

Learn more

Avoid

Buy now

Limited offer

Act now

Don't miss out

---

# Service Descriptions

Every service should answer

What is it?

Who is it for?

Which problem does it solve?

What outcome can be expected?

How do we work together?

Never begin with features.

Always begin with the customer's problem.

---

# Trust

Trust should be earned through

experience

clarity

expertise

consistency

transparency

Never through exaggerated claims.

---

# Language

Use

clinical language

professional terminology

modern dentistry vocabulary

digital workflow terminology

Avoid unnecessary buzzwords.

Explain technical concepts when needed.

---

# Structure

Every page should follow this sequence

Problem

↓

Understanding

↓

Solution

↓

Benefits

↓

Trust

↓

Call to Action

---

# Reading Experience

Users scan first.

Use

meaningful headings

lists

whitespace

visual hierarchy

highlighted key ideas

Avoid long uninterrupted text.

---

# Final Principle

Every page should leave the visitor thinking

"This company understands my daily work."
